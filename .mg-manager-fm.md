Director's reconciliation of the FM26 premiere study, the FM design-DNA
research, and the owner's addendums, into build orders. Owner's Addendum 2
names THIS file the governing UX document: where this conflicts with the
build spec or Addendum 1, THIS WINS. Where it is silent, the spec stands.
Authority order: Addendum 2 › this file › Addendum 1 › build spec.

Self-contained: everything a builder needs is restated here — no other study
document is required. Ships in the same two files:
`gibson/web/static/mg-manager-data.js` (generated by `tools/build_mg_data.py`,
never hand-typed) and `gibson/web/static/mg-manager.js`. Harness:
`tools/mg-harness.js`.

**The one law over everything: ZERO ENGINE CHANGES.** `MG_ENGINE` ships
verbatim from the spec ([ENGINE] §1–7): same pipeline, same rng order, same
record shape, same quirks, same worked examples. Every design below is data,
career logic, or presentation. The [BALANCE HOOKS] engine gates (1–8) stand
untouched.

**What is KEPT, verbatim:** the rebuilt animated match presentation in
mg-manager.js — build-up pass sequences, team surge, momentum tug-of-war,
the goal freeze ("the freeze is the roar"), banner pops, the 4-line
commentary feed, the FINAL MINUTES pulse. It becomes the renderer for
PLAY THE MATCH ▶, fed by `simulate()` records exactly as the spec's [UX
MATCH] section already rules (playback, not live Math.random — the visuals
and pacing are what's kept, the coin flips move into the engine). The
instant-result ticker below is an *alternative route into POST-MATCH*,
never a replacement.

## What FM's soul is (why these changes and no others)

The premiere and the design literature agree: FM is not the 3D match. It is
(1) an inbox that makes a database tick feel like personal news, (2) real
decisions between whistles — selection, fatigue, morale, counter-picks —
and (3) the standing offer to *not watch* ("you don't even have to show the
games"). Text is theatre of the mind; imperfect information makes choices
gambles you personally judged; the save is an alt-history you author. That —
not menus, not transfers — is what this build imports.

# 3. THE CARDS — 980 × 7 = 6,860 combos (+ the Legends shelf)
=====================================================================

**A card is a (player, parallel) pair.** Both dimensions are collectible;
owning white Messi and blue Messi is owning two cards. The binder counts
"213 / 6,860".

Generation (`tools/build_mg_data.py`, per Addendum 1 — unchanged):
- `config/sets/panini_wc2026_us.yaml` → 48 countries × 20 cards + FWC
  section (CC excluded). Names, teams, numbers from the shop's own truth.
- `config/prices/panini_wc2026_us.yaml` → base OVR on a log scale ($1 floor
  → 68–74, Messi's $18 → ~91), ~40-name star-override table so famous names
  always rate right (the spec's 20 verbatim rows are the core of it —
  ovr/sho/dfn/quirk/colours ship as authored there).
- Position from checklist number: **#1 = the country's CREST card** (POS
  `CLUB`, never fieldable — see §7.4 for what it does), #2 GK, #3–8 DEF,
  #9–14 MID, #15–20 ATT, star-position overrides apply. FWC cards are POS
  `FWC`, collectible-only, whimsy welcome ("the trophy itself. It does
  nothing. It's the trophy.").
- sho/dfn derived by the spec's stat-generation rules from OVR + position.

**The Legends shelf**: the unlockables and legends NOT in the 2026 album —
maradona, zidane, wemby, dort, caruso, phoenix, gibson, ellis, grandpa —
are extra cards beyond the 980, exactly as authored in the spec's verbatim
rows. They live on the LEGENDS wall, join pack pools only per their spec
gates, and are stitched into CPU country squads where §4 maps them.

**Multi-tier ownership rules (Addendum 2, made precise):**
- *The higher plays.* TEAM screens list each PLAYER once, at the best owned
  tier (tier ring shows it). Training targets a specific owned combo,
  chosen in the binder.
- *Dupes are exact-combo.* Pulling a combo you own converts to coins:
  spec band value × (1 + tierIdx) — a white 70 dupe pays 15, a black 90+
  dupe pays 840. Pulling an un-owned lower tier of an owned player is a
  KEEP (it's a new card — that's the fun). "UPGRADED!" free-upgrade
  ceremony is retired (superseded by combo ownership); the pack reveal
  stamps either "NEW CARD!" or "SWAP +N 🪙".
- *Training into a tier you already own* completes the upgrade AND
  instantly pays the now-duplicate combo's coin value ("SWAP" stamp rides
  the training ceremony).
- **Apps are per-player and cumulative** (amendment — per-copy apps die
  with multi-tier). One meter per player: competitive starts, never reset.
  Tier gates check the meter: blue 2 · red 5 · orange 9 · purple 14 ·
  green 20 · black 28 (+ Black Licence). Kid-legible: "play more matches
  with him." Coin costs unchanged (60/150/350/750/1500/3000).
- Energy, form, and morale (`bs`) are **per player**, not per combo — the
  sticker doesn't get tired, the player does.

Starter collection: 14 generator-chosen host-nation cards (USA / MEX / CAN),
all white, ≤84, no stars-overridden 85+, no quirks, quota GK×2 DEF×5 MID×4
ATT×3. 300 coins. (Whimsy: the family club starts with the hosts' spares.)

# 5. THE CAREER — World-Cup-shaped campaigns
=====================================================================

A career = endless CAMPAIGNS (was: seasons). One campaign, the GIBSON CUP:

- **49 teams, 48 slots**: each campaign one tier-1 country "couldn't make
  it this year" (seeded pick; kind copy) and the family club is invited as
  the WILD CARD team. News card at campaign start — pure whimsy, zero
  mechanics.
- **Group stage**: 12 groups of 4, drawn from strength pots (seeded,
  deterministic). You always occupy a pot-3 slot: every group has one
  giant, one peer, one minnow — underdog drama by construction. 3
  matchdays, single round robin.
- **Advance**: top 2 per group + the 8 best third places ("8 lucky thirds
  sneak through!") → 32.
- **Knockouts**: qualifiers re-seeded 1–32 by group record (pts, GD, GF,
  tier, index) into a fixed bracket, 1v32 down — kid-legible ("do well in
  the group, get a kinder road"), deterministic, no best-thirds bracket
  algebra. R32 → R16 → QF → SF → FINAL, shootout on draws.
- **Rounds**: r1–3 group, r4–8 knockout. 8 player matches max. Eliminated
  (group exit, or a knockout loss): the rest of the tournament resolves
  instantly and the WORLD screen tells the story — missing out must be
  visible (spec law) — then the ceremony.
- Round flow stays ATOMIC: your fixture completes (watched or ⏩), CPU
  round resolves, round++, one save write. Abandon-a-match rule unchanged
  (nothing recorded, `attempt++`). On load, always the hub.

**CPU results are DERIVED, never stored.** All CPU fixtures sim at
`attempt 0`, seeds `h = (campaign.seed ^ (round*2654435761) ^
(slot*40503)) >>> 0` (slot = match index in round), so any table, bracket,
scorer list or news detail is re-derivable byte-identically on demand.
Session cache; ~24 sims per group round, trivial. Only YOUR results are
saved (≤8 rows). This is also the news engine (§7.1) and the optional
Golden Boot.

**Two summits** (preserving the spec's two-different-summits ruling):
- *First Gibson Cup win* → Maradona (`cup-champ`, unchanged text:
  "Champion of Champions").
- **Second Gibson Cup win → the BLACK LICENCE** (was: league title).
  Locked-button copy: "Win the Gibson Cup TWICE to earn the Black
  Licence." Still permanent, still the only road to training black, black
  still `maxBlackInXI: 1`.

**Campaign payouts** (replaces seasonPos + cup tables; per-match coins,
matchFeats, streaks, friendly caps all unchanged):
```
economy.campaign = {
  groupPos: [150, 100, 60, 30],                    // paid at ceremony
  reached:  { r32:40, r16:70, qf:110, sf:160, final:220 },  // cumulative
  champion: 400,
  goalMet:  100 }                                   // §7.5 campaign goal
```
A champion's perfect run ≈ 1,050 + ~640 match coins; a median (55%)
manager out in the R16 ≈ 700–1,100 per campaign — the harness gate (§11).
Campaigns are shorter than the old season, pay proportionally, and end in
ceremonies more often: right for kids, right for phones.

Ceremony (review) order, one `env.over` per campaign, unchanged in spirit:
final standing + bracket story → payout count-up → goal grading (§7.5) →
feat/unlock beats → Licence beat when earned → optional GOLDEN BOOT card
(derived) → history/trophies/lifetime → campaign n+1 (fresh seed).
`progression.formationsUnlockSeason` now counts campaigns.

## 7.1 THE INBOX — "NEWS DAY" (the spine)

Flow: `POST-MATCH → CONTINUE → NEWS DAY (2–4 cards) → HUB`. One card at a
time; tap/Enter advances; **Esc (or the SKIP chip on phones) clears the
whole day in one press** — news NEVER blocks, the one FM gate we refuse.
An envelope chip joins the top chrome left of the coins (≥48×48, unread
badge); tapping it re-opens the day's cards as an overlay from HUB.
No new hub tile.

Storage: `profile.news = [campaignN, roundN]` (last round seen). Item ids
deterministic (`n{c}-{r}-{type}`); every word derived from state + banks —
nothing else stored. `demoMode` never generates news.

Card order (cap 4/day):
1. **GROUP ROUND-UP** (rounds 1–3) / **AROUND THE CUP** (knockouts): your
   group's other score, plus one shock line from elsewhere (margin ≥3 or a
   lower-tier winner). Byline gag: "— The Sticker Times". One recurring
   pest question per campaign, never answerable ("Settle, Nelly.").
2. **PROGRESS** (max 1; priority order): TRAINING READY ("Ellis can train
   to RED — 150 🪙", fires when apps + coins both satisfy, highest-eff
   eligible first) or FEAT WATCH (any counter exactly 1 from a feat —
   stateless check).
3. **SCOUT REPORT** (group round 2, and EVERY knockout round): scouts the
   next opponent (§7.4) — names their genuinely best card at current
   scaled tier + one honest quirk line from their `scout` bank. Signed
   "— the scout"; after `season-done`, "— Grandpa" (copy swap only).
4. **NEXT UP** (always last): crests, stakes footer derived from the table
   ("win and you top Group C"), and the opposing manager's taunt —
   `taunt[floor(seededFlavor*3)]`, seeded `campaign.seed ^ round`, so it
   never flickers.
One-shots: WILD CARD invite + CAMPAIGN GOAL (campaign start), GOLDEN BOOT
(ceremony, optional).

Render: centered card `rr(160, 90, 640, 420, 18)`, turf2, gold edge;
headline ≤5 words 26px; body ≤2 lines 16px; left column art (crest 64px,
`drawChar` of the taunting manager at 1.4× with speech bubble, or the card
renderer). Pips "2/4". 160ms slide; `sfx.move` per advance. All copy rules
apply (≤70 chars, one sentence, excitable, never mean).

## 7.2 INSTANT RESULT — "GET THE RESULT ⏩"

TACTICS keeps everything specced and splits kick-off into two ≥48×48
buttons: **PLAY THE MATCH ▶** (the kept animated presentation) and
**GET THE RESULT ⏩**. Both call the same `simulate()` with the same seed —
mode changes presentation, never outcome. The WORLD screen's SIM button
routes to TACTICS with ⏩ pre-focused: even when not watching, the manager
still manages (mentality/formation is the FM soul and is never skipped).
`demo(t)` shows both modes. FRIENDLY and DRAFT DUEL always play animated —
spectacle is their point; ⏩ is a career feature.

THE TICKER (no chrome, match family): scoreboard chip top-center; up to 6
moment rows at `y = 150 + i*62` — minute disc (34px), one line from the
EXISTING commentary banks (events already carry `flavor`), running score
chip right; FT row; shootout compresses to one `PENS 4–3` row + the
winning kick's line.

Key-moment selection (deterministic, zero rng):
```
must = every goal | pen_goal | hand_of_god          // every score change shows
fill = until ≥4, in order: pen_saved/pen_over/pen_caught/ruled_out/
       phoenix_catch, saves min ≥75, posts, big_tackle
if >6: trim fills only — musts never trim (a 7-goal match shows 7)
sort by minute
```
Pacing: whistle t=0; moment i at `0.8 + i*1.1s`; `sfx.point` yours /
`sfx.thud` + small shake theirs; FT +0.6s; auto to POST-MATCH +1.2s; total
≤8.5s. First tap reveals all instantly, second continues.

**Honesty invariants (state in code comments):**
1. Pressing ⏩ COMMITS the match immediately — Esc fast-forwards, never
   abandons (else it's a peek-and-reroll fishing tool; PLAY's abandon rule
   unchanged).
2. One shared `settleMatch(record)` settles coins/feats/counters/energy/
   form/morale for both modes — watching never pays more.
3. Selection consumes no rng, stores nothing; same record ⇒ same ticker.

## 7.3 MORALE — one face per card

No new engine channel: a pure display function + one gentle driver into
the existing form channel (bounded ±3% via `formMult` — [BALANCE HOOKS]
untouched). Visible, single-cause, always fixable — the anti-FM-hidden-
variable, in FM's own words-not-numbers style:

```
mood(player):                       // eff = active (best-owned) tier eff
  energy === 0            → DIZZY    "RESTING"        fix: sit him out a round
  form === +1             → BEAMING  "ON FIRE"
  eff ≥ 85 && bs ≥ 3      → SULKY    "WANTS TO PLAY!" fix: start him next match
  form === −1             → SAD      "COLD"           fix: needs a goal or a win
  else                    → HAPPY    "READY"
```
Sulk driver, after each competitive round: started ⇒ `bs = 0`, else
`bs = min(bs+1, 9)`; then after the normal form pass: if `eff ≥ 85 &&
bs ≥ 3 && form > −1` ⇒ `form −= 1`. One start resets everything through
existing rules. Friendlies touch nothing. Only eff ≥ 85 cards sulk —
rotating the mid squad for energy is never punished. Save: one pruned int
per player (`bs`).

`drawFace(ctx, x, y, r, mood, t)` — procedural, drawChar-styled: gold disc
`#ffd98a`, ink `#2a1f16`. BEAMING ∩∩ eyes + wide smile + rosy dots ·
HAPPY dots + smile · SAD dots + down-arc + one sweat drop · SULKY angled
brows + flat mouth + puffed cheek · DIZZY X eyes + wavy mouth + 2 stars
orbiting on `t`. Replaces the form arrow everywhere (battery pips stay —
energy and mood are different questions): r=7 face on TEAM discs, 22px
face + label word + one-line fix on the card sheet; NEWS DAY may reference
it ("Kane is SULKING on the bench").

## 7.4 SCOUTING — the mask fits, kept light

A country is SCOUTED when any of: you have faced it in ANY competitive
match (persisted), you own its CREST card (the #1 album card earns its
keep: "the badge tells you everything" — permanent scouting), or the
SCOUT REPORT covered it this round.

Masked (unscouted) opponents: TACTICS banner shows strength as "??" and
hides their mentality (so no informed RPS counter-pick — scouting IS the
edge); manager taunts still show (taunts are free). WORLD screen shows "?"
strength stars on unscouted rows. The mask affects DISPLAY ONLY — sims
never read it (harness-asserted). No ranges, no scout errors, no staff —
FM's uncertainty at one-glance size.

## 7.5 CAMPAIGN GOAL — FM's club vision, miniature

At campaign start (news one-shot + WORLD header): a derived target from
your squad's strength rank among the 48 — rank ≤4 "WIN IT" · ≤10 "reach
the SEMIS" · ≤20 "reach the QUARTERS" · ≤32 "reach the knockouts" · else
"win a match". Graded at the ceremony: met → `+economy.campaign.goalMet`
and a proud line; missed → a kind forward look, NEVER a penalty, no
sacking, no board. One pure function + copy.

## 7.6 THE BINDER — 6,860 cards on a 960×600 canvas

Replaces SQUAD/CARDS. Never renders more than a page:
- Filter chips (≥48px): TEAM (48-flag picker overlay + LEGENDS + FWC),
  POS, TIER; sort eff desc default (toggle: newest). Pages of 18 (6×3),
  tap arrows / Left-Right to page. No text search — no keyboards on
  phones; filters suffice.
- **ALBUM PAGE view** (the shop's soul): pick a country → its 20 slots in
  checklist order, owned combos bright with tier pips, missing ones as
  silhouettes — the real album, in miniature. Crest slot shows the §7.4
  perk. Completion chip "13/20".
- Binder header: "SO FAR: 213 / 6,860". Card sheet unchanged from spec
  (ladder strip, TRAIN button, disabled-reason lines) + morale face + the
  cumulative-apps meter ("played 3 — blue needs 2 ✓").

## 7.7 Small polish beats (premiere-sourced, all copy/presentation)
