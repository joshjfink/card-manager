# CARD MANAGER v2 — AUTHORITATIVE BUILD SPEC
Game director's reconciliation of the five specialist designs (economy, engine,
career, UX, content). Where they conflicted, the ruling is here and final.
Files: `gibson/web/static/mg-manager-data.js` (pure data, loaded first) and
`gibson/web/static/mg-manager.js` (engine + career + UI). Nothing else ships.
A dev-only node harness lives at `tools/mg-harness.js` (not referenced by
index.html — it loads both files onto a fake `window = globalThis`).

## OWNER'S ORDERS — where each lands (all preserved)
- Deep football-manager career → [CAREER]: 8-club league + Gibson Cup + CPU
  parallel scaling + training/apps + energy/form + feats + two profiles.
- Nine unlockables (Ellis, Gibson, Grandpa Fink, Phoenix, Maradona, Lu Dort,
  Caruso, Wembanyama, Luis Suárez) → [CAREER §feats].
- Parallel = 25% of effective skill, base = 75%, Haaland > Pulisic at any
  equal parallel → [ENGINE §1] formula + tie-break + harness asserts.
- Training raises parallel step by step → [CAREER §training].
- Hot-seat stays → two career profiles, Friendly quick match, Draft Duel.
- FC-27-STYLE ratings authored for this shop, never EA data → honesty markers
  in [UX §copy] and the data file's header comment.
- No gambling shape: packs cost match coins only (the owner's own words:
  "earned with match coins, never bought"), no real money, no purchase UI, no
  nag copy, honest odds text on the shop screen.

## DIRECTOR'S RULINGS (the conflicts, decided)
1. **Parallel score table**: ENGINE/CONTENT's `{white:40, blue:50, red:60,
   orange:70, purple:80, green:90, black:99}` WINS. Economy's 60–78 table is
   rejected: one step there is +0.75 eff (~0.004 pGoal) — a kid could never
   feel a training upgrade, which kills the core loop. One step is now
   +2.5 eff (~3.3 base points). Consequence: **black Pulisic (87.75) beats
   white Haaland (78.25)** — the ladder genuinely matters — and black Grandpa
   (77.25) still loses to white Haaland, which is correct and funny. The
   economy spec's "Haaland white > Pulisic black" assertion is superseded; the
   owner's only verbatim law (equal parallel ⇒ base order) holds at every
   colour by construction.
2. **One currency**: coins only. TP is cut (UX, CONTENT, CAREER are all
   coins-only; two currencies is too much for a seven-year-old). Duplicates
   convert to coins — or free-upgrade the owned card if the pulled parallel is
   higher.
3. **Packs cost coins** (UX/CONTENT model), per the owner's verbatim line.
   Economy's award-only triggers ("every 3rd win") are cut; prize packs
   survive as feat rewards and cup/title awards. Pack parallel ceiling is
   PURPLE; green is training-only, black is training-only AND licence-gated.
4. **Career macro-shape**: CAREER's single 8-club league + Gibson Cup + CPU
   parallel scaling WINS. Economy's 5-division pyramid and UX's 10-club/18-week
   league are cut (divisions triple club data; CAREER's fixture table, seeds
   and atomic rounds are the most build-ready design).
5. **Clubs**: CONTENT's themed clubs WIN over CAREER's trait clubs (they carry
   the unlockables and real squads the engine needs anyway). CAREER's seven
   engine-side trait modifiers (cheat/bottler/bully/…) are CUT as over-scoped:
   club personality is delivered through authored squads, fixed
   formation+mentality, the quirks the engine already has, and manager lines —
   zero new engine branches. `wallfc` is cut (8 CPU clubs don't fit a
   you+7 league; its identity merges into Hoop Dreams — Wemby IS "The Wall").
6. **Quirks**: ENGINE §7's table and probabilities WIN verbatim (CONTENT's
   0.30-forced-save numbers would wreck goal rates). Ellis/Gibson/Grandpa/Yamal
   get NO mechanical quirks — their personality lives in commentary banks
   (engine spec's explicit design). CONTENT's nutmeg/stepover/sit-down
   mechanics are cut; the lines survive as commentary.
7. **Black source**: train green→black (3000 coins, 8 apps), gated on the
   **Black Licence** (win the league title once, permanent). UX's
   "paint any card black" token is cut — it breaks the owner's step-by-step
   ladder rule. Black never in packs. `maxBlackInXI: 1` (one-of-one lore).
   Maradona's feat is the CUP (economy/career), so league and cup are two
   different summits.
8. **Abandon-a-match**: CAREER's rule WINS — nothing is recorded,
   `season.attempt` increments so the replay is a fresh honest match. UX's
   stored-record replay (`pendingMatch`) is cut.
9. **Tactics surface**: `{formation, mentality}` only. UX's FEED-THE-STAR /
   SHARE-IT row is cut — the engine contract has no such input and adding one
   reopens balance. (Shooter selection already favours high-`sho` finishers,
   see ruling 10.)
10. **sho/dfn**: the sim runs on E(p) alone (engine spec verbatim) with ONE
    addition: the shooter-pick weight is multiplied by `(effSho/70)`, so
    Haaland out-shoots a same-E defender. This changes WHO shoots, not how
    many chances or goals occur — goal-rate targets stand. `dfn` is card-face
    display only (defensive identity = slot role + quirks). Derivation is
    additive: `effSho = clamp(sho + (eff − base), 1, 99)`, same for dfn;
    round for display only.
11. **Starter collection**: 14 cards (CONTENT's count), all white, all base
    ≤84 (economy's cap — so Kane is NOT a starter), no unlockables, no
    legends: quota GK×2 DEF×5 MID×4 ATT×3, which fields every formation
    legally with a bench. 300 starting coins.
12. **Coin scale**: CONTENT's match table + CAREER's season/cup structure
    (doubled to fit training costs) — see [CAREER §economy]. Economy's
    hot-seat calendar caps and weak-opponent factor are cut; the only farm
    guard needed is the friendly cap (fixtures give no opponent choice).
13. **One arcade entry**: the v1 snake-draft game folds into the career build
    as FRIENDLY → DRAFT DUEL (same `id:'manager'` registration; two files
    only). It still ends with `env.over` exactly as v1 did.
14. **Feats for the nine**: economy's set WINS (it's a difficulty ramp and
    matches the owner's names one-to-one); CAREER's check machinery (post-match
    vs season-review) is kept. Ellis and Gibson are unlockable per the owner's
    verbatim list — but they land in the first hour by design.

# [ENGINE] — window.MG_ENGINE (pure, headless-safe)
=====================================================================
`window.MG_ENGINE = { effSkill, simulate }`, defined in mg-manager.js above
all UI code, reading tables only from `window.MG_DATA`. No Date.now, no
Math.random, no DOM, no env.*, own clamp/weighted-pick helpers. All
randomness through the injected `rng() → [0,1)` in the fixed order below;
same inputs + same seed ⇒ byte-identical records.

## 1. Effective skill — THE 75/25 RULE (load-bearing)
```
score(parallel) = MG_DATA.parallels[idx].score      // 40..99 table above
effSkill(p) = 0.75 * p.base + 0.25 * score(p.parallel)     // keep float
```
Tie-break wherever two players/teams compare equal: higher base wins, then
alphabetical id (deterministic; guarantees Haaland ≥ Pulisic outcomes).
Worked examples (harness must reproduce exactly):
```
Haaland 91 white  = 68.25 + 10.00 = 78.25
Haaland 91 blue   = 80.75          (one step = +2.5 eff ≈ 3.3 base pts)
Pulisic 84 white  = 73.00 ; Pulisic 84 black = 63.00 + 24.75 = 87.75
→ equal colour: Haaland leads Pulisic by 5.25 at ALL SEVEN colours (REQUIRED)
→ black Pulisic 87.75 > white Haaland 78.25 (the ladder matters)
Grandpa 70 black  = 77.25 < white Haaland 78.25 (base still rules; funny)
Messi 92 black    = 93.75 > Maradona 95 white 81.25 (ladder matters at the top)
```
Display stats: `eff` shown as round(effSkill); `effSho = clamp(sho + (effSkill
− base), 1, 99)`, same for dfn — round for display only.
In-match value, computed once at kickoff per player:
```
fatigueMult = energy>=60 ? 1.00 : energy>=30 ? 0.92 : 0.85
formMult    = 1 + 0.03*form                        // form ∈ {-1,0,+1}
oopMult     = roleOf(p.slot)===p.pos ? 1.00 : 0.85
E(p) = effSkill(p) * oopMult * fatigueMult * formMult
```

## 2. Contract
```
MG_ENGINE.simulate(squadA, squadB, opts, rng) → record
player: { id, base, pos, slot, parallel, energy, form, sho, dfn, quirks:[] }
squad:  { name, formation, mentality, penaltyTaker:null|id,
          starters:[9, slots matching formation, exactly one GK],
          bench:[0..4, no slot] }
opts:   { knockout:false }        // true ⇒ shootout on a draw
```
Validates 9 starters / slot counts / one GK; throws on illegal input (the UI
never lets an illegal lineup reach kickoff). The career layer builds CPU
lineups deterministically: best E per slot from the club's 12, club's fixed
formation/mentality, energy 100, form 0.

## 3. Kickoff numbers
```
edge(mine,theirs): defensive beats attacking, balanced beats defensive,
  attacking beats balanced.
  winner:  { create:1.10, convert:1.06, prevent:1.00 }
  loser:   { create:1.00, convert:1.00, prevent:0.94 }
T.create/convert/prevent = mentality table × edge, computed once.

CREATE_W = { GK:0.05, DEF:0.30, MID:1.00, ATT:0.60 }
creationSum(T) = Σ starters E(p)*CREATE_W[roleOf(slot)] * T.create
attSupport(T)  = mean E of ATT-slot players
defAvg/midAvg/keeperE analogous
defenceQ(T) = (0.55*defAvg + 0.10*midAvg + 0.35*keeperE + 2*(nDEFslots-3)) * T.prevent
```
Recompute team numbers if a knock removes a player.

## 4. Match loop (rng order is part of the spec — never reorder)
```
pMoment = 0.115 * (tempoA + tempoB)/2
shareA  = CA^1.3 / (CA^1.3 + CB^1.3)
for minute 1..90:
  minute 46 → emit half_time
  rng() < pMoment → side = rng() < shareA ? A : B → resolveMoment(minute, side)
emit full_time; if drawn && opts.knockout → shootout
```
resolveMoment — shooter pick first (ruling 10's one change from the engine
spec, in bold):
```
weight(p) = E(p) * (ATT 1.00 | MID 0.45 | DEF 0.10 | GK 0) * (effSho(p)/70)
shooter = one rng() over cumulative weights
```
Then the pipeline, each step consuming rng only if reached:
```
STEP 1 BIG TACKLE: if any defending DEF/MID has big_tackle (take highest-E):
  rng()<0.10 → chance dies, emit big_tackle;
    then rng()<0.15 → emit knock (family-safe), shooter off, auto-sub best-E
    bench same-role else best-E bench (OOP) else play with 8; recompute teams;
    emit sub if one came on.  return.
STEP 2 PENALTY: pPen = defending mentality=='defensive' ? 0.05 : 0.035
  rng()<pPen → emit penalty_awarded, resolvePenalty(side), return.
STEP 3 HANDS: shooter has suarez_hand && rng()<0.10 → emit ruled_out, return.
  shooter has maradona_hand && rng()<0.04 → GOAL, emit hand_of_god, return.
STEP 4 SHOT:
  Q = (0.70*E(shooter) + 0.30*attSupport(side)) * side.convert
  D = defenceQ(other)
  pGoal = 0.30 + (Q-D)/120  ; +0.08 haaland_ice ; -0.05 giant_keeper faced
  clamp(0.05, 0.65)
  defending keeper has phoenix_catch && rng()<0.10 → emit phoenix_catch, return
  rng()<pGoal → GOAL, emit goal {player, quirk?}
  else r=rng(): r<0.45 save (giant_keeper: rng()<0.60 tags quirk:'wemby')
       | r<0.65 post | else wide
```
resolvePenalty(side):
```
taker = penaltyTaker if on pitch, else highest-E ATT, else highest-E outfielder
kane_pens && rng()<0.30 → emit pen_over, return
p = clamp(0.74 + (E(taker)-E(keeper))/200, 0.55, 0.90)
kane_pens → p += 0.10 ;  haaland_ice → p = 0.85 ;  giant_keeper → p -= 0.08
keeper phoenix_catch && rng()<0.20 → emit pen_caught, return
rng()<p → pen_goal else pen_saved
```
Shootout (knockout draws): 5 highest-E on-pitch outfielders best-first
(designated taker first if on pitch), 5 rounds A-then-B via resolvePenalty's
formula, stop when decided; sudden death down remaining players (GK last) then
cycle. Every kick emits shootout_kick {round, side, player, scored};
`record.shootout = { score:[a,b], kicks:[…] }`.

## 6. Record (exact shape)
```
{ version:1, score:[2,1], shots:[7,5], onTarget:[4,3], chances:[8,6],
  scorers:[[ids],[ids]],
  events:[ {min, side, type, player?, quirk?, flavor} ],   // flavor = one rng()
  shootout:null|{score,kicks},
  ratings:{ id:8.4,… }, motm:'haaland',
  fatigue:{ id:-30|-15 }, knocks:[ids], subs:[{min,side,off,on}],
  meta:{ avgEff:[a,b], trailed:[bool,bool] } }   // kickoff mean E per side;
                                                 // did each side ever trail
```
`meta` is the one addition to the engine spec's record — the feat layer needs
it (hustle, comeback-win, giant_kill) and it's pure kickoff/derived data.
Ratings: start 6.0; +1.2/goal; +0.4/shot on target saved; keeper +0.5/save or
catch, +0.8 clean sheet; +0.6/big_tackle; +0.3 every winning starter; clamp
4.0–10.0, round 0.1. MOTM = highest, one rng() tie-break (last rng consumed).
Energy deltas: played −30, subbed on −15; the career layer applies them, plus
+40 (cap 100) for sitting out a round. Form applied by career layer after each
competitive match: scorers/MOTM/clean-sheet GK → +1; starters on a losing side
not covered above → −1; everyone else one step toward 0.

## 7. RNG
mulberry32 (engine-spec reference implementation) is the generator for sims,
pack pulls and the harness. Seeds: `season.seed` rolled once per season
(`(Math.random()*2**32)>>>0` — the only Math.random in the file); per match
`h = (season.seed ^ (round*2654435761) ^ (slot*40503) ^ (attempt*97)) >>> 0`
(slot 0–3 in round; cup: round 15 semis / 16 final, slot = tie index; CPU
fixtures attempt 0). Pack pulls use `h = (profileSeed ^ packsOpened*2654435761)`.

# [CAREER] — league, seasons, economy, feats, save
=====================================================================
## Shape
One career per profile, endless seasons. A season: 14 league rounds (8 clubs,
double round robin, 56 matches) → Gibson Cup (top 4: semis + final, single
leg, shootout on draws) → Season Review → next season. No relegation, no fail
state — a bad season pays less and earns Wooden Spoon commentary. Squad,
coins, parallels, apps, unlocks, licence, trophies, history persist; table,
fixtures, cup, per-season stats reset. Depth = squad building + CPU parallel
scaling + club-linked unlocks + energy/form rotation + the trophy record.

Round flow is atomic: the player's fixture completes (watched at
`economy.matchSeconds` (40s default, speed chip ×1/×2/×4) or SIMmed — same
`simulate` call), then the round's three CPU fixtures resolve headlessly,
round++, one save write. Table/cup/next-fixture are DERIVED from
`season.results` + fixtures on every render — never stored. Points 3/1/0;
sort: pts desc, GD desc, GF desc, head-to-head pts desc, lower index last.
Abandoning mid-match records nothing; `season.attempt++` so the retry is a
fresh honest match. On load the career always opens at the hub.

Cup: created atomically with round 14's results. Semis seed1v4, 2v3 (higher
seed home), final neutral; player ties interactive-or-simmable; if the player
missed the top 4, the whole cup resolves instantly and is shown as one
screen — missing out must be visible.

CPU scaling at season start: every CPU card's parallel = its season-1
authored rank + min(season.n−1, 4) steps, capped at purple for tier-1/2
clubs, green for tier-3/4/5 — except Maradona, black always, and no other
CPU card ever reaches black. The player's squad is never auto-scaled.

# [BALANCE HOOKS] — what tools/mg-harness.js must measure
=====================================================================
Run ≥10k seeded sims per check; all thresholds are ship gates.
1. **Determinism**: same seed twice ⇒ deep-equal records; any rng reorder
   fails this first.
2. **75/25 exactness**: for every player and colour,
   `|effSkill − (0.75*base + 0.25*score)| < 1e-9`; full ladder span =
   0.25×59 = 14.75 eff — the parallel's share is exactly 25%, by
   construction, and the assert proves nobody drifted it.
3. **Haaland/Pulisic law**: effSkill(Haaland,c) > effSkill(Pulisic,c) for
   ALL seven colours; black Pulisic > white Haaland; black Grandpa < white
   Haaland; reproduce every worked example in [ENGINE §1] to the cent.
4. **Equal mirrors**: two identical 75-E balanced squads ⇒ win/draw/win
   ≈ 37/26/37 (±3) — win-rate vs equal squads ~50%.
5. **Goals**: mean combined goals 2.4–3.4 across mirror mentality setups
   (inside the owner's 2–4 band); no configuration averages >9.
6. **Mentality RPS**: counter-pick at equal squads worth +8..12 win-prob
   points.
7. **Ladder worth**: same XI all-black vs all-white wins ≥80%; each single
   full-squad parallel step adds a measurable, monotonic +4..8 win-prob
   points vs the unstepped mirror (kids must FEEL one training step).
8. **Quirk sanity**: Kane over 5000 pens ≈ 66% scored; suarez_hand goals
   ruled out ≈10% of his chances and NEVER score; exactly ≤1 hand_of_god
   per ~25 Maradona chances.
9. **Career difficulty curve**: Golden Oldies (t1, season 1) beats the
   starter XI 25–40% of sims; Legends XI beats an all-white 85-avg squad
   ≥75%; a full-green 90-avg squad beats season-1 Legends XI ≥55% (the
   ladder is climbable); season-6 scaled Legends XI still loses ≥40% to a
   mixed green/black endgame squad.
10. **Economy pacing**: scripted-career sim (median manager: 55% win rate,
    buys packs greedily) earns 1,200–1,700 coins/season; full white→black
    ladder on one card affordable by season 5–7; day-one coins (300) buy
    exactly a bronze pack + change toward blue training.
11. **Pack odds**: 100k seeded pulls per pack converge to the rating and
    parallel tables ±1 point; zero green/black pulls ever; zero locked
    unlockables/legends outside their gates; bronze pity honoured.
12. **Content validation**: roster quotas/bands exact; all ids unique and
    ≤11 chars; every club squad id exists and meets quota; every commentary
    placeholder ∈ {p,k,team}; every quirk id has a bank and a mechanic;
    every feat reward id exists; starter collection fields all three
    formations legally.
